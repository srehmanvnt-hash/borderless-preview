Clinical Trial Match

A free search engine that matches you to eligible clinical trials — using your own words. You don't need a complete molecular pathology report. Enter what you know, and we'll show you which trials to look at, and what to ask for next.

Not a clinical decision. This tool is decision support for you and your treating team. It reads published trial criteria against the information you provide. It is not medical advice, and nothing here replaces a conversation with your oncologist. Trial status changes weekly — always verify with the study team before travelling or screening.

Tell us about you

The more you can tell us, the better the match. Leave anything blank if you don't know it — we'll flag what's missing and tell you which tests would open the most options.

Don't worry about medical vocabulary. Plain language works — we'll pull out the key facts. Do not include information that identifies you (name, address, date of birth).

What to obtain next

Always confirm with the study team. Registry records are summaries. A verdict of "met" or "unknown" means only that the public record does not disqualify you — it does not mean you are eligible. Your treating oncologist's judgement comes first.
