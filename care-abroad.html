What we help with

Whether you're seeking a second opinion, a specialist procedure, or sequencing for you or your family, the information you need is in one calm place.

Verified hospitals and clinics

Facilities cross-referenced against national medical registries and accreditation bodies — with research background and patient reviews highlighted, so you can compare like for like.

Clinicians and specialists

Individual clinicians checked against their relevant medical board — licence current, specialty matching, and any public restrictions disclosed. Reviews from real patients, not marketing copy.

Genomic sequencing labs

Private labs offering consumer and patient sequencing, with plain-language explanations of what each test can and cannot tell you — aligned with approved clinical guidance.

Independent providers

We don't only list famous Western centres. We help excellent independent providers around the world reach patients who need them — and support local economies while doing so.

Reviews that mean something

Moderated by people, not algorithms. Reviewers confirm they received care. Providers cannot pay to remove or reorder reviews. Patterns are flagged, not single anecdotes.

Language support

Every provider listing shows the languages spoken by the clinical team, and whether interpreters are available. Language is a barrier we take seriously.

How we verify

Every provider goes through the same four checks before listing. We re-verify on a schedule, and immediately if we become aware of a change.

1. Registry check

GMC, state medical boards, or the relevant national authority. Licence current, specialty matching, no undisclosed restrictions.

2. Credentials and research

Qualifications, fellowships and research activity checked against institutional pages and, where available, peer-reviewed publications.

3. Facility standing

Accreditation against JCI, ISO 15189 (for labs) or national equivalents. We review whether the facility publicly reports its outcomes.

4. Patient experience

Patient reviews and testimonials, moderated by people. We flag patterns, disclose the good and the difficult alike, and never allow a provider to edit reviews.

Grounded in approved guidelines

Where we publish information about conditions, tests or treatments, we align it with approved clinical guidance from recognised bodies — NICE (UK), FDA (USA), EMA (EU), WHO and national regulators. We link to the source so you can read it yourself. We do not offer our own medical opinion, and we will never advise against the advice of your treating clinicians.
