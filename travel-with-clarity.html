A real question we hear often: "UK, South Korea or Thailand?"

Here's the same case laid out plainly. There isn't one right answer — the right answer is the one that fits your passport, your energy, your language and your budget.

Hardest access, highest ceiling

United Kingdom

Embassy visa · 10h flight · highest cost tier · deepest specialist expertise. Usually the right choice for very complex cases, and the wrong choice if the wait is too long.

Strong alternative, shorter flight

South Korea

e-Visa · 11h flight · mid cost tier · advanced oncology and surgical programs. Good for patients who want a high ceiling without a Western wait.

Easiest access, gentlest pace

Thailand

Visa on arrival · 6h flight · low cost tier · strong private hospitals with English-speaking teams. Often the right choice for second opinions and less complex treatments.

Please note: Destinations and visa status shown here are indicative, not a guarantee. Visa rules change, and so do costs. The routes shown are the most favourable for the citizenships and residence selected — we confirm every requirement with you personally before any travel or booking, and if a route we've shown isn't right for you, we'll say so.
