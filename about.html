Care shouldn't stop at a border.

Borderless Health was founded by two people who have been through cancer treatment — and who know how much stands between a patient and the care they need.

Why we exist

We have been patients in the UK and the USA. We have seen how differently two excellent health systems can feel from the inside — and how much of the hardest work falls not on the doctors, but on the patient.

The searching. The waiting. The referrals that go nowhere. The forms, the phone calls, the second-guessing at 2am. The realisation that the care you need might exist, but it is on the other side of a border you were not prepared to negotiate.

We built Borderless Health because nobody should have to do that work while they are unwell. Our purpose is simple: to make care across borders clearer, calmer and more honest — so that patients and their families can spend their energy where it matters most.

"We built what we wished had existed when we needed it. If it spares even one person the worst parts of what we went through, it will have been worth it." — The founders of Borderless Health

What we believe

Four principles guide every decision we make — about what we build, what we list, and what we refuse to do.

Your doctor comes first. Always.

We are not a medical provider and we do not give medical advice. Every decision about your care belongs with you and your treating clinicians.

Honesty, even when it costs us.

If we can't help, we'll say so. If a provider isn't a good fit, we'll say so. If a partner pays us a commission, we'll disclose it.

Grounded in approved guidelines.

We align our information with approved clinical guidance from recognised bodies — NICE, FDA, EMA, WHO and national regulators.

Choice for everyone, everywhere.

Access to excellent care shouldn't depend on where you were born, where you live, or what passport you hold.

Our services

Everything we offer is built around the same question: what would have helped us, at the moment we needed it most?

Verified care abroad

Hospitals, clinicians and genomics labs around the world — checked against official registries, enriched with patient reviews, and shown alongside the languages spoken by the clinical team.

Clinical Trial Match

A free search engine that matches patients to eligible clinical trials using their own words — and shows what to obtain next, in order of leverage.

Travel with Clarity

Honest, practical guidance on getting to your destination. We help patients understand visa requirements based on their citizenship(s) and country of residence, compare regional and global hubs, and navigate the process with less hassle.

Regional guidelines, by condition

How a specific condition is treated in different countries, drawn from approved clinical guidance — so patients can compare approaches with their treating clinician.

Second opinion portal In development

A structured way for patients to seek a second opinion from a verified specialist abroad, with clinical information handled carefully.

Wellness journeys

Nature-based retreats chosen for genuine restorative value — designed to support physical and mental wellbeing.

A free basic will

A free basic will-writing service, so that planning ahead doesn't have to start with cost.

What we are — and what we are not

We are a verified directory of hospitals, clinics, clinicians and genomics labs; a source of patient reviews and approved-guideline information; a clinical trial matcher; a second-opinion service in development; a wellness travel partner; and a starting point for basic estate planning.

We are not a medical provider, a diagnostic service, or a substitute for your treating clinicians. We will never advise against medical advice. We will never sell your health information. And where we cannot help, we will say so.
