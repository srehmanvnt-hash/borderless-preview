About Borderless Health

We help patients and consumers find verified healthcare abroad — and we make the path to it clearer, calmer and more honest.

Why we exist

Seeking care abroad is often harder than it needs to be. The information is scattered. The quality of providers is hard to verify. Visa rules, language and cost vary from country to country. And the burden of piecing it all together falls on the patient — usually at a time when they have the least energy to spare.

Borderless Health exists to remove that burden. We verify providers against official medical registries. We surface patient reviews that mean something. We map visa access, language support and regional hubs so patients can see their real options. And we ground everything we publish in approved clinical guidance, because empowerment should be built on evidence, not opinion.

What we believe

Four principles guide every decision we make — about what we build, what we list, and what we refuse to do.

Your doctor comes first. Always.

We are not a medical provider and we do not give medical advice. Every decision about your care belongs with you and your treating clinicians. Our job is to make information clearer — never to override clinical judgement.

Honesty, even when it costs us.

If we can't help, we'll say so. If a provider isn't a good fit, we'll say so. If a partner pays us a commission, we'll disclose it. Trust is the only currency that matters in health.

Grounded in approved guidelines.

We align our information with approved clinical guidance from recognised bodies — NICE, FDA, EMA, WHO and national regulators — so that what you read reflects established practice, not opinion.

Choice for everyone, everywhere.

Access to excellent care shouldn't depend on where you were born, where you live, or what passport you hold. We help patients explore options across borders, and we help independent providers and local economies benefit from that choice.

Our services

Verified care abroad

Hospitals, clinicians and genomics labs around the world — checked against official registries, enriched with patient reviews, and shown alongside the languages spoken by the clinical team.

Care Passport

An interactive tool that shows patients where they can travel for care, based on their citizenship(s) and country of residence — with the easiest visa route for each destination and both regional and global hubs side by side.

Regional guidelines, by condition

How a specific condition is treated in different countries, drawn from approved clinical guidance — so patients can compare approaches with their treating clinician, rather than relying on fragments of information online.

Second opinion portal In development

A structured way for patients to seek a second opinion from a verified specialist abroad, with clinical information handled carefully and the advice returned to the patient and their treating team.

Wellness journeys

Nature-based retreats chosen for genuine restorative value — designed to support physical and mental wellbeing, and to support the local communities in which they operate.

A free basic will

A free basic will-writing service, so that planning ahead doesn't have to start with cost. Advanced services are available through vetted partners, with clear disclosure of how we work with them.

What we are — and what we are not

We are a verified directory of hospitals, clinics, clinicians and genomics labs; a source of patient reviews and approved-guideline information; a second-opinion service in development; a wellness travel partner; and a starting point for basic estate planning.

We are not a medical provider, a diagnostic service, or a substitute for your treating clinicians. We will never advise against medical advice. We will never sell your health information. And where we cannot help, we will say so.
