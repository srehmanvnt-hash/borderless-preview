A free basic will, to start

Protecting what matters shouldn't cost anything to begin. Our free basic will service helps you take the first step — because planning ahead is part of looking after yourself and the people you love.

What's included

A basic will covers the essentials: who receives your estate, who looks after any dependants, and who carries out your wishes. It's the right starting point for most people — and it's free.

Your estate

A clear statement of who should receive what — money, property, and personal belongings.

Guardianship

For parents and guardians: an opportunity to name who would care for your children if you were unable to.

Executor

The person who will carry out your wishes — a family member, a friend, or a professional.

Peace of mind

Your wishes written down clearly, so the people you love are not left guessing at a difficult time.

If you need more than a basic will

Some situations need more than a basic will — trusts, complex estates, business interests, cross-border assets, or advice on inheritance tax. Where that's the case, we'll say so honestly and connect you with a vetted partner. We disclose clearly how we work with them, and we will never push you towards a paid service you don't need.

Complex estates

Trusts, multiple properties, business holdings or cross-border assets.

Inheritance tax

Advice on how to structure an estate to minimise tax exposure for your beneficiaries.

International considerations

If you hold assets in more than one country, or have family in different jurisdictions.

What this service is — and is not

This is a free basic will-writing service, and a starting point for planning ahead. This is not legal advice for your specific circumstances. For complex estates, or where the law in your country requires a solicitor or notary, we will recommend you consult a qualified professional. Where we work with partners, we disclose all commissions and fees before you engage with them.

Questions?

If anything here is unclear, or you're not sure whether a basic will is right for you, just ask. We'd rather over-explain than have you guess.
